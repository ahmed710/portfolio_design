Process / Figma + AI

From Figma frame to production

Every product above started as frames on a canvas. I design the flows myself, use AI design tools to widen the options early, and hand off specs I then implement.

  1. Map the flowUser journeys and low-fidelity wireframes for every screen.
  2. Explore with AIGenerate several layout and style directions with AI design tools, then keep the strongest ideas.
  3. Build the systemComponents, variants and auto layout in Figma, shared by web and mobile.
  4. PrototypeClickable prototypes to validate flows with the business before writing code.
  5. Hand off and shipSpecs from Figma into Angular and Flutter components, wired to Spring Boot, .NET or Firebase.
